NEXCORE R16 MINI APP LAUNCHER HOTFIX

Changes:
- Ordinary players default to Mini App-only mode once MINIAPP_URL or RAILWAY_PUBLIC_DOMAIN exists.
- MINIAPP_ONLY_MODE=0 remains the emergency switch back to the legacy Telegram UI.
- Administrators now see "Открыть Nexcore" at the top of their admin home keyboard.
- /miniapp command opens the Mini App launcher for admins/testing.
- No database files, migrations, schema versions, assets, user data, or event data are changed.

Required Railway networking:
- The service must have a public HTTPS domain.
- Either let RAILWAY_PUBLIC_DOMAIN be provided by Railway or set MINIAPP_URL explicitly.
